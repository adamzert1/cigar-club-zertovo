About

Who we are

JMM Ventures works in property, construction and local business on two continents: in the United States and in the Czech Republic.

In the US we manage property, handle new construction and provide notary services in Florida. In the Czech Republic we manage property through Správa Levne, build and remodel, buy properties to fix and flip, and run businesses with deep local roots, from the Žertovo orchards to our farm shop on Karlovo náměstí in Prague.

The businesses support each other. A remodel crew, a property manager, an accountant and a cleaning team work better under one roof than four separate contracts.
