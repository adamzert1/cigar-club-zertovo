About

Who we are

JMM Ventures is an independent, privately owned company. We start our own projects and partner with founders whose work we want to see grow.

We are hands-on owners. Alongside capital we bring operating experience, a network of trusted partners, and the patience to let good ideas mature.

Because we invest our own money, we can move quickly, keep decisions simple, and judge success over years rather than quarters.
