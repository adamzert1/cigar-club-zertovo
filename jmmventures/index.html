About

Who we are

JMM Ventures is a privately held group active in real estate, construction and agriculture on both sides of the Atlantic.

In the United States we operate primarily in Florida, where we focus on property acquisition, new construction and property management, and provide notary public services. For European and American clients we also advise on trusts, probate, tax filings and estate planning. Florida offers clear property rights, a strong economic outlook and a stable environment for long-term investment.

In the Czech Republic we manage residential property through Správa Levně, carry out construction and renovation work, and acquire properties for renovation and resale. We also operate the Žertovo brand: family orchards in Bříství near Kolín and a farm shop on Karlovo náměstí in Prague.

Our businesses are designed to work together. Property management, construction, cleaning and accounting under one group means fewer handovers, consistent standards and a single point of responsibility for our clients.
