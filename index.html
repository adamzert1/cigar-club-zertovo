The Club

A small club with a big table.

Tucked away at Bristvi Křečhoř 18, Cigar Club Žertovo is where members gather to light a good cigar, open a rare bottle, and let an evening unwind the way it should — without a clock in sight.

We carry the spirit of the Žertovo family: the same dotted tree, the same red, green and black, the same belief that the best moments are shared around fire and food. But the club stands on its own roots — a place built for cigars, rum, and the people who appreciate both.

SmokeA relaxed lounge for slow cigars and long conversation.
SpiritA growing cellar of collectible and tasting rums.
FeastOpen-fire BBQ, special nights and seasonal feasts.

“We don’t rush the good things. We light them, pour them, and pass them around.”

What We Do

Nights worth leaving the house for.

The calendar at Žertovo runs on appetite and good humour. These are the gatherings our members come back for, season after season.

Signature

Open-Fire BBQ

Our special barbecue nights — low, slow and over real flame. Big cuts, bigger plates, and a cigar to follow once the coals settle.

Specialty

The Badger Night

Our most talked-about specialty event — a one-of-a-kind feast and tradition that newcomers have to experience to believe. Ask a member.

After Dark

Strawberry Daiquiri Nights

When the weather turns warm, the blender comes out. Fresh strawberry daiquiris, rum from our own shelves, and music until late.

Seasonal

Seasonal Feasts

Long communal tables, shared dishes and the whole club in one room. Our feasts mark the turning of the seasons the proper way.

Cellar

Rum Tastings

Guided pours from the club’s collection — agricole, aged, navy-strength and the odd rare find. Notes shared, bottles compared.

Every Week

Cigar Lounge Evenings

The heart of it all — quiet evenings in the lounge with a cigar, a dram and good company. No occasion required.

The Rum Cellar

A collection that keeps growing.

Rum is more than a drink at Žertovo — it’s a shared project. Members hunt, trade and pour bottles from across the rum-making world, and every find earns its place on the shelf.

Agricole

Grassy, fresh-pressed cane spirit for the purists.

Aged & Dark

Barrel-rested bottles for slow, after-dinner sipping.

Navy Strength

Bold, high-proof rums for the daiquiri blender and the brave.

Rare Finds

Single casks and limited bottlings shared on special nights.

“Every bottle has a story — and a night it was opened.”

The Rhythm of the Club

How a season unfolds.

No two months at Žertovo look quite the same, but the club keeps a comfortable rhythm. Here’s the shape of it.

Weekly

Lounge Evenings

The doors are open, the humidor is stocked, and members drift in for a cigar and a quiet pour.

Monthly

Rum Tasting & BBQ

A guided flight from the cellar, paired with our open-fire barbecue once the coals are ready.

Summer

Strawberry Daiquiri Nights

Warm-weather sessions with fresh fruit, club rum and music that runs late into the evening.

By Tradition

The Badger Night

Our signature specialty event — held when the time is right, and never forgotten by those who attend.

Each Season

The Grand Feast

One long table, the whole club, and a meal to mark the turning of the year.
